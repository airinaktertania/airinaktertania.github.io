Research Interests

Exposure-Aware Vision

Deep learning for low- and high-light image enhancement, single-shot exposure correction, and object detection under difficult lighting.

Geometry-Preserving Networks

Riemannian representation learning and equivariant networks on the SPD manifold for structured, covariance-based data.

Biomedical Signals

EEG-based emotion recognition, time–frequency analysis, and attention–graph fusion models for physiological data.

Generative Modelling

Conditional flow matching, difficulty-calibrated interpolation paths, and efficient sampling for generative image models.

Research Experience

Undergraduate Thesis Researcher — Dept. of EEE, KUET

Jan 2023 – Feb 2024

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Mohiuddin Ahmad

  • Developed deep learning architectures for joint low- and high-exposure image enhancement, culminating in the AutoLumNet framework [J1].
  • Proposed a bi-branch, exposure-aware design coupling unsupervised correction with perceptual optimisation, removing the need for paired exposure supervision.
  • Built and curated a custom multi-exposure dataset; designed the evaluation protocol and ran full-resolution benchmarking against state-of-the-art baselines.
  • Led manuscript preparation, figure design, and citation auditing for submissions to IEEE TPAMI and IEEE QPAIN.

Independent Research Collaborator — Riemannian Learning & Generative Modelling

2024 – Present
  • Designed equivariant network layers on the SPD manifold with geodesic prototype heads for end-to-end classification of covariance-structured EEG and vision data [C1].
  • Developed difficulty-calibrated interpolation paths for conditional flow matching, improving sample quality under constrained sampling budgets [C2].
  • Implemented and benchmarked models in PyTorch across multi-GPU environments with reproducible, released research code.
